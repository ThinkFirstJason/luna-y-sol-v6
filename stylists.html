The stylists

Two chairs, one obsession

Luna y Sol is built on one belief: curly and wavy hair isn't something to be managed. It's something to be celebrated.

Curl specialist

Hector

Hector is Luna y Sol's resident curl specialist, with a signature dry-cutting approach that transforms clients' relationships with their natural texture. Warm, patient, and deeply knowledgeable, he makes every appointment feel like a masterclass in your own hair.

Cut & color specialist

Sarah

Sarah has built a devoted following over years of cuts and color that work beautifully in real life, not just in the chair.
