Welcome to the studio

Luna y Sol is a small, boutique studio on Colorado Boulevard, built around curly and wavy hair. Most salons shy away from texture. We specialize in it.

Sit down, exhale, and tell us about your hair. We'll take it from there.

What we do

From dry-cut curly transformations to lived-in balayage, everything we do is tailored to your texture.

  1. 01 Curly & Wavy Cuts Our specialty Dry cutting · Curl consultation · Styling lesson
  2. 02 Cuts & Styling For every texture Precision cuts · Blowouts · Updos · Special occasions
  3. 03 Color Lived-in & luminous Balayage · Highlights · Single process · Gloss & toner
  4. 04 Texture Services Transform or smooth Perms · Brazilian blowout · Smoothing
  5. 05 Extensions Length & volume Application · Maintenance · Texture matching

The signature

Golden Goddess Curls

We shape every curl dry, one at a time, so the cut follows your pattern instead of fighting it. Then we warm it up with soft, sun-kissed color that grows out without a harsh line.

The result: spring on day one, and still on day five.

  • Cut dry, curl by curl
  • Lived-in golden color
