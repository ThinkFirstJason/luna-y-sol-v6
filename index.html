Welcome to the Studio

Luna y Sol is a small, boutique studio on Colorado Boulevard, built around curly and wavy hair. Most salons shy away from texture. We specialize in it.

Sit down, exhale, and tell us about your hair. We'll take it from there.

Services

From dry-cut curly transformations to lived-in balayage, everything we do is tailored to your texture.

  • Curly & Wavy Cuts

    Our specialty

    The cornerstone of everything we do. Using the dry-cutting method, we sculpt each cut to the unique character of your curls, maximizing spring, minimizing bulk, and giving you a shape that wears beautifully on day one and day five.

    Dry cutting, Curl consultation, Styling lesson

  • Cuts & Styling

    For every texture

    Precision cuts for all hair types, from blunt bobs to layered lengths. We also offer blowouts and special-occasion styling, from polished updos to soft, undone texture.

    Precision cuts, Blowouts, Updos, Special occasions

  • Color

    Lived-in & luminous

    Lived-in, sun-kissed color that moves the way you do. Balayage, highlights and gloss placed to grow out softly, without a harsh line.

    Balayage, Highlights, Single process, Gloss & toner

  • Texture Services

    Transform or smooth

    We offer perms for clients who want waves and curls, Brazilian blowouts, and professional smoothing treatments for frizz-free, manageable hair.

    Perms, Brazilian blowout, Smoothing

  • Extensions

    Length & volume

    High-quality extension application and ongoing maintenance for added length, volume, or both.

    Application, Maintenance, Texture matching
